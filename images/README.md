# Photos for Sisonke

Each photo slot shows an on-brand illustration until you add a real photo here.
Name the files exactly as below (JPG, ~1600px wide, under 300 KB each) and they fade in automatically:

| File          | Where it shows            | What to look for                                   |
|---------------|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| hero.jpg      | Top of the home page      | Portrait-ish photo of people meeting around a table |
| steps.jpg     | "How it works"            | Someone checking a phone, portrait                 |
| burial.jpg    | Burial societies card     | Family or community supporting each other          |
| stokvel.jpg   | Savings stokvels card     | Friends or neighbours gathered, counting or talking |

Free-licence sources (check each photo's licence page, and credit the photographer in the table below):
- https://unsplash.com/s/photos/south-african-community
- https://unsplash.com/s/photos/african-women-together
- https://www.pexels.com/search/south%20africa%20community/
- https://www.pexels.com/search/african%20family/

Use real South African community photos where you can. Avoid images of identifiable private people
unless the licence is clear, and never use photos of real members without their consent.
